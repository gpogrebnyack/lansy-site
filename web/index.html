For today

What to fix first thing

Morning

Refunds and compensation go through the duty manager only

A hard stop for today. The desk said: Well, I think we’ll refund this night, I’ll just decide it now. We’ll sort it out with the manager later. Don’t name an amount or promise a refund date. Log the complaint, give a time for the answer, hand it to the duty manager and come back to the guest with a decision.

🛡️ Brand riskFront deskSafety
Morning

Bring empathy back into every complaint

There was a solution but no warm first step: Sorry, yes, about the charger, that we didn’t call back straight away. Better: “I understand how frustrating it is to wait for news about your item. I’m sorry we didn’t call back right away — let me check and come back with a clear answer.”

Turn information into bookings

The shift talked about services but rarely booked them: We have a lovely studio, they run workshops there. Next step: “Tomorrow the Kids Club has a sand-art workshop, AED 150 — shall I book a place? And while they’re busy we can arrange a babysitter, so the evening is yours for the spa.”
